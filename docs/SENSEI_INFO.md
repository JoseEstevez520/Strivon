# What Sensei told us

Notes from the talk with Sensei on 10/10.

## Stock levels

What they said:

- `empty` is when you would go and restock. Their example was having one or two units left.

How we read the rest:

- `half` is everything in between.
- `full` works like `empty` the other way round: if only one or two units are missing, it is still `full`.
