# Contributing

- Work on your own branch, named `name/topic`, and open a pull request to `main`. Someone else takes a quick look and merges it.
- Commit with your own account. Small commits, often.
- Commits follow Conventional Commits: `type(scope): description`. No co-author or AI
  attribution lines.
- Everything in the repo is in English: code, comments, docs, commits, PRs and issues.
- Text a person will read is written plainly, the way a person would say it.
- Folders: `backend/` is the Python pipeline, `frontend/` is the React app, `eval/` is
  scoring and our own labels, `docs/` is the plan and the task split, `assets/` holds logos
  and README images. `data/` and `outputs/` are never committed.
- A change that makes the score worse does not go in.
