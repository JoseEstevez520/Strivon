# Pieces

Six ready-made pictures at 16:9, each telling one idea, to use as they are in a slide or as an
example of how to compose one. They use the page colour `#f8f7f4` and the model's answers
(`outputs/predictions.json`) for the last photo of each camera. The camera photos are the
challenge's, reduced, and the chat answer uses sample data.

| File | Size | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `piece-1-photos-to-store.png` | 1920×1080 | Camera photos with their marks, a small 3D store with four gondolas, and the stops of the round numbered as in the 3D |
| `piece-2-one-gondola.png` | 1920×1080 | One camera photo with the model's marks, and what is wrong in that gondola in words |
| `piece-3a-store-3d-4-gondolas.png` | 1920×1080 | The 3D store alone, four gondolas |
| `piece-3b-store-3d-14-gondolas.png` | 1920×1080 | The 3D store alone, all fourteen gondolas |
| `piece-4-what-the-model-sees.png` | 1920×1080 | Three crops of one photo: empty, wrong product and fine |
| `piece-5-chat-answer.png` | 3840×2160 | The chat's answer drawn from the app's own parts: a day heat map and a gondola card |

`index.html` shows them all in one page.
