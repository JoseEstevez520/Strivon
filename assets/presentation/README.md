# Presentation material

Pictures and clips of Strivon for slides, the landing and posts. The shelf states in them are the
model's answers (`outputs/predictions.json`, written by `agents/predict.py`) unless a folder says
otherwise.

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `views/` | Full pages of the app at 1920×1080 |
| `components/` | The app's parts cut out one by one, most with a transparent version |
| `graphics/` | Social images, diagrams, charts, mockups, brand pack and slide backgrounds |
| `pieces/` | Six finished pictures, each telling one idea, to use as they are or as examples |
| `kit/` | Parts drawn from scratch in the brand: chips, tiles, figures, matrices, devices, patterns, dividers and an annotation kit |
| `video/3d/` | The 3D store as a clip, a loop and a GIF |
| `identity/` | The brand pack: logo system, slot-field patterns, posters, slide covers, social images, stickers, motion and a brand sheet. Open `identity/index.html` |
| `slides/` | The story as an editable PowerPoint, one idea per slide |
| `web-deck/` | A presentation as a web page: eight slides, the restock wave as a transition, clips and a QR |
| `export/` | The web deck as a PDF, and the scripts that make PowerPoint files, pictures and an MP4 from it |

The challenge's photos appear reduced in `views/`, `components/`, `pieces/` and the clips.
