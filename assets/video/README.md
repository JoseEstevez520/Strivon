# Brand videos

Short clips of the chat for the pitch, the landing and social posts. They are recreations made
with Remotion, not screen recordings, so they play the same every time and cost nothing to redo.
The chat has no box around it, so what the answer shows is what you look at. The shelf states in them are
simulated, so the clips are for showing how the chat works, not as results of the model.

| File | Size | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `chat-bananas.mp4` | 1920×1080, 17 s | Ask how the bananas are doing, get a chart and a split, then ask where they sit |
| `chat-bananas-vertical.mp4` | 1080×1920, 17 s | The same clip for phones and social posts |
| `chat-restock-first.mp4` | 1920×1080, 12 s | Ask what to restock first, get the gondola to go to and the next three |
| `demo-tasks.mp4` | 1920×1080, 14 s | The app's first page: the day plays and the plan of the store fills with what needs doing, the round list orders the stops, then a stop opens its gondola with the shelf areas marked, and back |
| `chat-to-gondola.mp4` | 1920×1080, 17 s | Ask where the bananas are, get a chart of the day, open the card and land on the gondola with its shelf areas marked. It is the clip on the landing |
| `ad/strivon-ad.mp4` | 1280×720, 18.6 s | The brand ad, with voice and music: a bright supermarket, fresh produce, a ceiling camera, then the restock wave and the wordmark. No app on screen. The 1080p master (13.8 MB) is on the material site. `ad/strivon-ad-poster.jpg` is its poster |
| `phone/phone-round.mp4` | 1920×1080, 16 s | The staff round on a phone: start the round, load up, tick off three stops, round complete. A recreation with the app's real screens and icons |

The three chat clips also come as `*-alpha.webm`, with a transparent background, to
place over any page or slide. The text is dark, so use them on a light background.

`phone/step-1.png` to `step-6.png` are the six steps of the round as loose pictures with a transparent background, to drop on a slide.

`chat-to-gondola.gif` and `demo-tasks.gif` are light copies of those clips for the main README.

The ad and the phone round are made in the same Remotion project. It is kept outside this repo for now. The shelf photo in the
clips is one of the challenge's camera photos, reduced.
